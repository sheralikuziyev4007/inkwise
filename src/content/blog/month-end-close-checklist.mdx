## Before the month ends

The fastest closes start a week early. Chase missing supplier invoices, confirm accruals with budget owners and agree a cut-off time for purchase requests.

- Send a reminder to suppliers with open purchase orders.
- List recurring accruals that need sign-off.
- Lock the cut-off date in the shared calendar.

## The first three days

Focus on what blocks everything else: bank reconciliation, accounts payable and accounts receivable. Anything that arrives as a document — invoices, statements, receipts — should be in the system on day one, not day four.

<Callout title="Why documents slow the close">
  Unprocessed invoices are the most common reason a close slips. If they are still being keyed in by hand, that is where to automate first.
</Callout>

## Closing out

Review variances against budget, post the final journals and write a short note on anything unusual. Keep that note: next month's close is faster when this month's surprises are written down.
