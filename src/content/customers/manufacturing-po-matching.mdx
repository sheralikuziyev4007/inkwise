## The challenge

[COMPANY] buys components from more than [N] suppliers. Growth meant more purchase orders, and the procurement team was spending most of its week comparing PDFs line by line.

## The rollout

Inkwise was connected to the supplier portal and the ERP. Each incoming invoice was matched against its PO at line level — SKU, quantity and unit price — with tolerances the team defined.

}
