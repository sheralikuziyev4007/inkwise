## The challenge

Hallmere Components buys parts from more than 260 suppliers. Growth meant more purchase orders, and the procurement team was spending most of its week comparing PDFs line by line.

## The rollout

Inkwise was connected to the supplier portal and the ERP. Each incoming invoice was matched against its PO at line level — SKU, quantity and unit price — with tolerances the team defined.

```json
{
  "match": "full",
  "po_number": "PO-7731",
  "lines": [{ "sku": "A-40", "ordered": 40, "invoiced": 40, "price_delta": "0.00" }],
  "action": "auto_approve"
}
```

## The results

Nine out of ten invoices now match their PO without review. The remaining exceptions — mostly partial deliveries — reach the right buyer with the three values side by side, which saves the team about 22 hours a week.
