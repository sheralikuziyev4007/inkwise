## The challenge

Corvane Logistics moves freight for retailers across three countries. Every carrier, fuel supplier and warehouse partner sends invoices in its own format — PDFs by email, scans from depots, the occasional photo of a paper slip.

A finance team of six keyed every one of them into the ERP by hand. Month-end close waited on the last stragglers, and mismatches between delivery notes and invoices were usually found weeks later.

## The rollout

The team started with a single shared mailbox. Inkwise read every attachment, extracted vendor, totals, tax and line items, and matched each invoice to its delivery note.

Low-confidence fields went to a review queue instead of the ERP. Corrections made there were fed back, so the same vendor layout rarely needed a second look.

## The results

Within two months, 82% of invoices went from inbox to ERP without anyone touching them. The two people who used to type all week now spend Monday morning on exceptions, and the close moved from the ninth working day to the fifth.

<Callout title="What made the difference">
  Matching invoices to delivery notes before posting meant disputes were raised with carriers while the shipment was still fresh.
</Callout>
