## The challenge

Every new supplier to Velmora Health sends a stack of forms: legal entity details, tax identifiers, bank information and signed contracts. Missing fields meant days of emails before a vendor could be paid.

## The rollout

Inkwise extracted each form as it arrived and checked it for completeness. Missing or inconsistent fields triggered an automatic request to the supplier, and complete records were created in the ERP and CRM.

<Callout title="Sensitive data">
  Bank details were masked in the review queue and visible only to users with the finance role.
</Callout>

## The results

Average onboarding time fell from 12 working days to 4. Because incomplete forms are caught on arrival, the vendor team sends 70% fewer follow-up emails.
